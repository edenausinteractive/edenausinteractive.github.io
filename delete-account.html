Delete your account and data

Oath of the Three Kingdoms · Developer: Edenaus Interactive

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete My Data at the bottom of Settings.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted at once and the game starts again as new.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email edenausinteractive@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and your in-game name. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (guest or Google Play Games sign-in) and your player ID.
  • All game progress stored on our servers and on the phone: heroes, items, currencies, purchases made in the game, settings and your in-game name.
  • Your arena record, alliance membership and friend list.
  • Usage and error data sent for analytics (a deletion request is sent to Unity Analytics).

What may be kept

  • Payment records held by Google Play for your purchases (managed by Google under its own terms; we never see card details).
  • Messages you sent through Contact Us, kept up to 90 days so we can finish answering you, then deleted.

Deleting your account cannot be undone: purchases made in the game are lost with it.

删除账号与数据

游戏内：主页 设置 → 删除我的数据 → 确认，账号与数据立即删除。
邮件：发送至 edenausinteractive@gmail.com，标题“删除我的账号”，附上游戏名字，我们将在30天内删除并回复确认。

删除内容：账号与玩家ID、服务器及手机上的全部游戏进度（武将、道具、货币、游戏内购买、设置、名字）、竞技场记录、联盟与好友关系，以及分析数据。Google Play保留其支付记录；通过“联系我们”发送的消息最多保留90天。删除后无法恢复。
