Privacy Policy

Oath of the Three Kingdoms · Developer: Edenaus Interactive · Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what information Oath of the Three Kingdoms (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: edenausinteractive@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhyProvider
A player ID created when you play as a guest, or your Google Play Games player ID if you sign in with GoogleTo keep your progress and purchases and restore them on another phoneUnity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code); Google Play Games Services

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or messages outside the game, and we do not sell your information.

Your choices

  • Usage data: asked the first time you play; change it any time in Settings > Share Usage Data.
  • Ads: rewarded ads are optional. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), Google’s consent form asks before any ad; change it in Settings > Privacy Options.
  • Reminders: notifications can be turned off in Settings or in Android’s settings.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Delete My Data erases your progress on the phone and on our servers, and requests deletion of the analytics data. You can also email us.

Keeping and protecting information

Progress is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete it. Analytics data is kept for up to 25 months. Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by our service providers, who may process it in other countries under their own privacy terms: Unity, Google.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any change here, and in the game’s announcements for important ones.

隐私政策

我们收集的信息

  • 游客账号ID或Google Play游戏账号ID：用于保存进度与购买记录，并在新手机上恢复（Unity Gaming Services、Google Play游戏服务）。
  • 游戏进度（武将、道具、货币、设置）与你设定的名字：用于运行游戏及在线功能（排行榜、联盟、好友）。
  • 购买记录（订单号、商品、凭证）：用于验证并发放购买内容、防止欺诈。支付由Google Play处理，我们无法看到你的银行卡信息。
  • 匿名游戏数据与错误报告：仅在你同意后收集，用于改进游戏（Unity Analytics）。
  • 观看激励广告时的广告ID与设备信息：用于展示与衡量广告；个性化广告仅在法律要求的情况下经你同意后提供（Google AdMob）。
  • 你通过“联系我们”发送的消息（及你提供的电子邮箱）：用于回复你。

我们不会收集你的通讯录、位置、照片，也不会出售你的信息。

你的选择

  • 使用数据：首次游戏时询问，可随时在“设置 > 分享使用数据”中更改。
  • 广告：激励广告可自由选择是否观看；在法律要求的地区，观看前会先征得你的同意，可在“设置 > 隐私选项”中更改。
  • 提醒：可在设置或Android系统设置中关闭通知。
  • 删除数据：“设置 > 删除我的数据”会清除手机与服务器上的进度，并申请删除分析数据；你也可以发送邮件给我们。

儿童

本游戏不面向13岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集其信息。如发现儿童向我们提供了信息，请联系我们删除。
